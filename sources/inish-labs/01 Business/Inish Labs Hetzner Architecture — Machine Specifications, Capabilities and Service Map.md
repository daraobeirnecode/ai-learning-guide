# Inish Labs Hetzner Architecture — Machine Specifications, Capabilities and Service Map

> **Public-export privacy stub.** The private source documents a live personal agent environment and contains operational details, relationships, schedules, device identities or account-routing information. Those details are intentionally not republished.

## Public learning takeaways

- Separate personal, specialist and client contexts with explicit profile boundaries.
- Keep credentials outside notes and repositories; expose capability status, not secret values.
- Require explicit approval for messaging, external writes, deployment and destructive operations.
- Treat memory, scheduled work, plugins, skills and MCP servers as auditable configuration.
- Document recovery and verification without publishing private hostnames, accounts or contact data.
