## Filing rule

1. Capture uncertain material in the owning vault's Inbox.
2. Put durable explanation in Learning.
3. Put live architecture and runbooks in AI Systems.
4. Put outcome-bound work in Projects.
5. Put Inish Labs commercial material in the Inish Labs vault.
6. Keep raw sources separate from synthesis.
7. Keep one current starting note per subject; older versions point to it or move to Archive after review.

## Authority rule

A dated report, generated folder index, package-local README, or social capture is not automatically authoritative. Prefer the map's **Start here** or **Authoritative** section. Use the exhaustive cross-vault index only when the curated layer does not answer the question.
