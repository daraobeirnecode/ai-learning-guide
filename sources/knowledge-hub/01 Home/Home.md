## Find anything

1. **Known title:** use Quick Switcher.
2. **Exact phrase or property:** use Search.
3. **By status or area:** use the Bases views above.
4. **By relationship:** use backlinks from a trusted map or synthesis.
5. **By meaning:** use primary-agent semantic search after the current index rebuild.

## Vault rules

1. One root Home.
2. One canonical map per major theme.
3. One canonical synthesis per defined subject.
4. Sources preserve provenance; synthesis carries authority.
5. Project packages live in repositories/workspaces; Knowledge Hub stores decisions and knowledge.
6. Inbox items are processed, intentionally parked, or archived.
7. Machine-managed paths remain stable until their contracts are deliberately migrated.
8. No mass move, merge, archive, or deletion without a migration ledger and rollback point.
